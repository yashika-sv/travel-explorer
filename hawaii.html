🌺 About Hawaii

Hawaii is a group of beautiful islands located in the Pacific Ocean. It is famous for its stunning beaches, active volcanoes, lush rainforests, waterfalls and rich Hawaiian culture. Visitors can enjoy surfing, hiking, snorkeling and exploring the natural beauty of the islands.
