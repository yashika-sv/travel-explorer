🌺 About Maldives

The Maldives is a breathtaking archipelagic nation in the Indian Ocean, southwest of India and Sri Lanka. Made up of around 1,200 coral islands grouped into atolls, it is famous for crystal-clear turquoise waters, vibrant marine life, beautiful beaches and iconic overwater villas.
